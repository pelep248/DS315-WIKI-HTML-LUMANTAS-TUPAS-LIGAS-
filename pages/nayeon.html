Nayeon

  • Stage name: Nayeon

  • Birthdate: September 22, 1995

Nayeon is one of the 9 members of TWICE, the group under JYP Entertainment. She was born on September 22, 1995 in Myeongil-dong (Seoul), South Korea. She is the oldest member of the group.
