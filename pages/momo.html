Momo

  • Stage name: Momo
  • Birth name: Hirai Momo (平井 もも)
  • Birthdate: November 9, 1996
  • Birthplace: Kyoto, Japan
  • Nationality: Japanese
  • Position(s): Main dancer, Sub vocalist, Sub rapper

Momo is one of the 9 members of TWICE, the group under JYP Entertainment. She was born on November 9, 1996 in Kyoto, Japan. She is the main dancer of the group.

Quick facts

  • She was added to the group by JYP.
  • She is a member of the Japanese sub-unit MiSaMo.
  • Her birth name is Hirai Momo (平井 もも).

Role in TWICE

  • Main dancer
  • Sub vocalist
  • Sub rapper
