What is TWICE?

TWICE (트와이스) is a 9-member group under JYP Entertainment. The group was formed through the 2015 Mnet survival show Sixteen. They debuted on October 20, 2015 with the mini album The Story Begins and the title track "Like OOH-AHH". Their Japan debut was on June 28, 2017. The fandom name is ONCE.
