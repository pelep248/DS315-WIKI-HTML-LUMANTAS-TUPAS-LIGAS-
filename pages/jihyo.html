Jihyo

  • Stage name: Jihyo

Jihyo is one of the 9 members of TWICE, the group under JYP Entertainment. She was born on February 1, 1997 in Guri, South Korea. She is the leader and a main vocalist of the group.

Quick facts

  • She trained at JYP for over 10 years before debut.
