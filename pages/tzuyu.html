Tzuyu

  • Stage name: Tzuyu
  • Birth name: Chou Tzuyu (周子瑜)

Tzuyu is one of the 9 members of TWICE, the group under JYP Entertainment. She was born on June 14, 1999 in Tainan, Taiwan. She is the youngest member (maknae) of the group.

Quick facts
